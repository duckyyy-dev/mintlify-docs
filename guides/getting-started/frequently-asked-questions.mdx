## **General**[**Copy link to section**](https://bmrg.app/docs/getting-started/frequent-questions#general)

| **Question**                                                           | **Answer**                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| :--------------------------------------------------------------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| How do I invite Swift to my server?                                    | Click [here](https://bmrg.app/invite) to invite Boomerang to your server.                                                                                                                                                                            |
| Where do I access the dashboard?                                       | You can find our dashboard [here](https://bmrg.app/dashboard).                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| I bought premium, how do I enable it on my server?                     | You can manage your premium subscription in your [account settings](https://bmrg.app/account).                                                                                                                                                       |
| My server does not show up when I try to enable premium. What do I do? | Make sure the bot is in the server before attempting to activate premium. You can invite the bot [here](https://bmrg.app/invite).                                                                                                                    |
| I found a bug, where can I report it?                                  | You can report bugs in our [Discord server](https://bmrg.app/support) through our support channel. If the issue is sensitive or involves a potential security vulnerability, please email us at [contact@bmrg.app](mailto:contact@bmrg.app) instead. |
| How can I join the Boomerang team?                                     | If applications are open, you’ll see a posting on the [career page](https://bmrg.app/careers), and you’ll also receive a notification in our [Discord server](https://bmrg.app/support).                                                             |
| What is the difference between Legacy Premium and Premium?             | Legacy Premium is our name for the premium plan given to those who bought the bot before we moved to a monthly subscription model. There is no difference between Legacy Premium and Premium.                                                        |
| Can my server affiliate with Boomerang?                                | Boomerang does not offer nor intend to offer an affiliation program.                                                                                                                                                                                 |
| What is the Boomerang prefix command?                                  | We currently do not offer prefix commands.                                                                                                                                                                                                           |
| Can I customise my bot’s server profile?                               | Customisation is a way for our premium subscribers to customise the appearance of the bot on your server. You can edit the bot’s name, profile picture, banner and about me.                                                                         |
